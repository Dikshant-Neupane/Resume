About Me

Short Bio

A meticulous craftsman of digital experiences, bridging the gap between historical design principles and modern interactive media. I believe in the quiet power of intentional white space and the tactile sensation of well-set typography.

Education

2018 - 2022

BFA Interaction Design

2022 - 2024

MDes Graphic Design

Career Goals

To architect design systems that prioritize focus over distraction. My ambition is to lead a studio dedicated to slow-consumption digital products—interfaces that respect the user's time and aesthetic sensibilities.

Motivation

"The details are not the details. They make the design." — Charles Eames

I am driven by the legacy of print media. The permanence of ink on paper challenges me to bring that same level of rigor and lasting quality to ephemeral digital screens.
